Real numbers, not guesses

NestQuest's AI never makes up a price. Every dollar comes from calculators built on published data: the National Association of Home Builders' 2024 construction cost survey, U.S. Department of Defense state construction cost factors (2026), and the International Residential Code. The app checks every answer before you see it.

  • 🗺️ Works nationwide Construction costs adjusted for all 50 states and Washington, D.C.
  • 🛡 Safe by design Guards against manipulation and off-topic requests.
  • 📐 Checked against your lot Setbacks, height, coverage and room-size rules.
  • 🌱 Sustainable options Solar, EV charging and efficient construction methods, priced in.
  • 🔎 Fully transparent See every agent's work and the exact AI cost of your plan.

Our founder

Navneet Gupta

Navneet earned a postgraduate degree in management from Delhi University. In 1998 he founded SNAP, Inc., which grew into a trusted provider of IT and learning management solutions for government, Department of Defense and commercial clients. He also mentors and serves on the boards of several companies in IT and marketing.

After years of success in technology, Navneet brought the same entrepreneurial spirit to custom home development and founded EngeeHomes LLC. His focus is personalized living spaces that blend modern luxury with timeless craftsmanship, built with precision, care and a deep understanding of each client's preferences.

Building with purpose. Navneet's passion is driven by planning for both profit and purpose: EngeeHomes builds homes at cost for low-income families, helping them lay the foundation for a more stable future. It's a value he has instilled in his team and hopes to leave as a lasting legacy.
